Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information Learn With Sarvesh ("we", "us") collects on ai-ready.learnwithsarvesh.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Our course platform at learnwithsarvesh.com has its own privacy policy, which applies once you log in there.

What we collect

  • Details you give us — when you request the free roadmap, we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number and, if you choose, your background.
  • Usage data — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site: pages viewed, buttons clicked, device and browser type, approximate location (city or country) and how you arrived. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.
  • Payment details — payments are handled entirely by Razorpay on their secure pages. We never see or store your card or bank details; we receive confirmation of the payment and the contact details you enter there.

How we use it

  • To send you the roadmap you asked for.
  • To contact you by email, WhatsApp or phone about the AI Engineer Ready program, webinars and related updates.
  • To give enrolled learners access to the program and the learner community.
  • To understand what works on the Site and improve it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Who else processes it

We use a small number of trusted services to run the Site, and they process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Netlify — hosts the Site and stores roadmap form submissions.
  • Google Analytics — site usage statistics.
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • YouTube — embedded videos, shown in privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Graphy — our course platform, once you enroll.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you are interested in or enrolled in our programs, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • You can stop WhatsApp or email updates at any time by replying "STOP" or by writing to us.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Site will still work.
